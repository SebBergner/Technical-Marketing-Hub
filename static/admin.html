Hub usage

Content access

A PTC Only demo (called Internal until 2026-10-28) shows its card, description and details to everyone, but its files open only for people whose groups have Access internal content (PTC employees by default, not Partners). A demo is PTC Only when its SharePoint folder is closed to partners, or when it is marked here or on its own page. The mark is kept in the Hub; marking a SharePoint demo also sets its Customer facing column to No, since a PTC Only demo is never customer facing.
